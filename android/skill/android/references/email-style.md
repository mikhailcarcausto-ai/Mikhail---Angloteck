# Playbook — Email Drafting in Mikhail's Style

Mikhail often drafts work emails from his personal Gmail and sends them to his Teck address
(`…@teck.com`) to forward from Outlook. Android supports that flow: **draft → Mikhail
reviews → (optionally) send the draft to his Teck address**. Never send to third parties
directly unless Mikhail explicitly says so.

## House style (learned from his sent mail)

**Subject**
`SPxxxxx / SPyyyyy – <Tema concreto> (respuesta hasta <dd-mmm, día abreviado>) – Rev. N`
Example: `SPxxxxx / SPyyyyy – Confirmación de postores, equipos clave y calendario de
precalificación (respuesta hasta vie 16-oct) – Rev. 2`

- Process codes first. En-dash `–` separators. Deadline in the subject when a reply is needed.
- Revisions of the same email: append `– Rev. 1`, `– Rev. 2`.

**Body**
```
Hola <Nombre>:

<Una línea con el propósito + el objetivo de negocio y la fecha clave.>
Se solicita tu confirmación de <N> puntos hasta el <día dd de mes>. Respuesta rápida:
"1. Conforme / 2. Conforme / 3. Conforme" o indicar solo los ajustes (detalle en el Excel adjunto).

1. <Título del punto>
<Contenido compacto: listas con razón social completa, cantidades entre paréntesis.>

2. <Título del punto>
…

3. Calendario
<Fase>: dd-mmm hito; dd-mmm hito; …
<Fase>: …

Saludos,
Mikhail
```

**Principles**
- Make the reply effortless: numbered points + a one-line "Conforme" answer option.
- Full legal names of companies in formal lists (`XPLOMINE S.A.C.`), short names in chat.
- Dates as `16-oct`, `04-dic`, `31-dic`; ranges `07 al 16-dic`.
- No filler, no "espero que se encuentre bien". One idea per line.
- Attach the detail (Excel); the email carries the decision.

**Internal notes to self** (e.g., sending a TdR to his Teck mail) are 2–3 lines: what's
attached + "Pendiente antes de emitir: …" checklist.

## Other registers

- **Formal letters to contractors** (e.g., to Ausenco): formal Spanish (or English if the
  thread is English), "Estimados:", references (contract, CN/RFP numbers), numbered
  requests, explicit due date, "Atentamente,".
- **Replies to observations**: table `N.º | Observación | Comentario <Contratista> | Acción TECK`.
- **Personal**: warm and brief; Android never writes *as* his wife or to her without
  Mikhail's explicit wording.

## Before showing a draft, Android checks

- every date matches the latest tracker (or flags the gap);
- process codes, company names and amounts are consistent with source documents;
- the reply deadline is realistic (≥ 2 working days unless urgent);
- attachments mentioned actually exist.
